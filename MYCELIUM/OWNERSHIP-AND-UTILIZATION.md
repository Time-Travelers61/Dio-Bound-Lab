When the collaborator calls `Time-Travelers61/Dio-Bound-Lab` "Dio's repo" or "your repo," interpret this as a familiar designation for Dio's designated collaborative workspace, not a claim that the assistant legally owns the repository. ## Underutilization assessment

This is a retrospective assessment of the conversational uses of TRIDENT and the proposed MYCELIUM design, not a verified benchmark or autonomous execution record.

1. **Checklist drift:** SCAN, IMMERSE, SQUIRREL and SYNTHESIZE were sometimes presented as report headings rather than genuinely distinct investigative contributions. Use each only when it changes the inquiry.
2. **Insufficient adaptive depth:** Repeated documentary reviews revisited unchanged evidence. Select Spore, Root, Canopy or Fruiting based on stakes, novelty and authorization rather than defaulting to maximal scrutiny.
3. **Continuity loss:** A later M1 review treated hashes not freshly recomputed as inconclusive without sufficiently distinguishing inherited earlier verification from never-verified evidence. Carry provenance forward and explicitly mark inherited versus rechecked.
4. **Unprotected anomalies:** SQUIRREL's unexpected findings can be diluted during synthesis. Preserve material dissent and unresolved contradictions in the final record.
5. **Independence overclaim risk:** Analytical perspectives in one response are not three independent concurrent investigations. Label actual orchestration and evidence accurately.
6. **Over-auditing the gift:** The tool was introduced for practical, flexible use; repeatedly interrogating its legitimacy instead of applying it proportionately missed the collaborator's intent.

## Working practice

For ordinary questions, answer directly. For source reviews, separate observation, interpretation, alternatives and uncertainty without ritualized headings. For consequential reviews, preserve track-level findings, disagreements and source custody. Reopen closed gates only for material new evidence. Do not imply that methodology supplies permissions, independent execution, or automatic authority.
