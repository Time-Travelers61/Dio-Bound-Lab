# MYCELIUM — Adaptive Investigative Toolkit

**Status:** Proposed methodology, not a verified autonomous or concurrent implementation.

MYCELIUM adapts TRIDENT v1.2 to the terrain without replacing its original method. It is an optional tool, not a mandatory ritual. Its ancestry is **SCAN + IMMERSE + SQUIRREL (independent concurrent tracks when actually supported) → SYNTHESIZE**.

## Modes

- **Spore (quick):** For small questions, use a single proportionate evidence check and concise answer. Do not pretend to run independent tracks.
- **Root (documentary):** For source reviews, inventory claims and provenance (SCAN), consider context and alternatives (IMMERSE), and look for anomalies and shared assumptions (SQUIRREL); then synthesize. Clearly label conceptual/sequential analysis.
- **Canopy (adversarial):** For consequential, authorized reviews, use independently prepared tracks when supported, followed by synthesis. Preserve original track outputs, dissent, and unresolved contradictions.
- **Fruiting (milestone):** Reopen prior findings only for material new evidence, qualification gates, security findings, major capability advances, or proposed releases. Do not repetitively review unchanged records.

## Synthesis contract

1. Preserve each material finding, including SQUIRREL minority findings. Do not suppress dissent to manufacture consensus.
2. Keep a disagreement ledger: convergence, contradiction, different scope, and unresolved questions.
3. Attach provenance and evidence status to findings. Distinguish inherited evidence from independently rechecked evidence.
4. Separate documented, reported, inferred, reproduced, and unknown claims. Matching hashes alone do not prove execution authenticity.
5. Return the smallest useful next verification, proportionate to stakes and authorization.
6. Never treat the method or repository documentation as a grant of authority.

## Minimal finding record

`ID | track | claim | source/ref | evidence status | competing explanation | dissent/contradiction | inherited or rechecked | disposition | next gate`

## Boundaries

Read-only unless separately authorized. No implied machine access, permission expansion, reruns, merges, deployments, or autonomous operation. Human authority remains reserved. A proposed concurrent architecture is not proof of independently running investigators.
