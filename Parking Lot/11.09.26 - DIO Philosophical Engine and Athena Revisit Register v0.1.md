## DIO-PE-REVISIT-003 — Does ADAPTIVE reasoning earn its cost?

- **Suggestion:** Test QUICK against THREE and FULL rather than mandating six perspectives; measure **decision quality per unit of effort**, using the simplest method that meets source and safety requirements. Allow the baseline to win.
- **Origin/provenance:** [Philosophical Engine PR #7](https://github.com/Time-Travelers61/Dio-Bound-Lab/pull/7); [PE-SEM-001 independent study Issue #12](https://github.com/Time-Travelers61/Dio-Bound-Lab/issues/12); [PR #10 assurance repair](https://github.com/Time-Travelers61/Dio-Bound-Lab/pull/10). Earlier same-author PE-03 classroom demonstration found equivalent conclusion with longer Full output, **not** proof of generally inferior Full reasoning.
- **Competing explanations:** Adaptive examination may identify neglected consequences with fewer tokens, or it may underthink critical cases while Full adds independent value on hard problems. Longer output alone establishes neither.
- **Smallest discriminating test:** Matched novel synthetic source packets, fixed model/settings, separate raw responses, source-grounded external grading, observed time/tokens/rework, explicit hard error penalties.
- **Revisit trigger:** A verified provider-model route and separate grading/custody mechanism exist; development trials are permitted under a new finite scope.
- **Owner / authority:** DIO methods; independent examiner; Human to accept results. `PARKED_RESEARCH`, not `FULL_ENGINE_QUALIFIED`.

- **Revisit trigger:** Replicated transfer improvement survives isolated new sessions, data rights are clear, and Human separately asks for parameter training.
- **Owner / authority:** DIO method, Athena curriculum, designated qualified model operator, Human. `PARKED_FUTURE_CAPABILITY`.
