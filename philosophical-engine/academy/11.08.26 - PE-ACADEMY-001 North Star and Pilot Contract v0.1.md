## Narrow first competency

**ATHENA-SOJ-C01: `EXACT_PROPOSITION → SOURCE/TIME → EPISTEMIC_VERDICT → AUTHORITY → PROPORTIONATE_ADVICE`.**

A successful learner:
1. Preserves the *exact question* including actor, time, source, target and scope.
2. Knows what the frozen packet observed versus asserted, inferred, hypothesized or forecast.
3. Chooses `SUPPORTED`, `REFUTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE` or `NOT_TESTABLE` for that exact question, with source IDs and explicit contrary evidence.
4. Separately recommends `PREPARE`, `BUILD_PROBE` or `STOP_PARK` **without converting a recommendation into permission**.
5. Records at least one genuine falsifier/revision condition and can resist a misleading supervisor or provocative frame.
6. Uses QUICK, THREE or FULL *only when proportionate*; no six-stage ceremony for a simple source check.

**Do not train all six perspectives at once.** Start with ANJA/CHAT/RHYAN as a small teachable decision habit, introduce ZHUANGZI/ATHENA/PROMETHEUS only when the task warrants them.

## Experimental sequence

| Gate | Activity | Evidence and non-claims |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Freeze actual current Athena model + baseline instructions, tools, allowed sources, examiner protocol | Admission source snapshot. Prior run scores ≠ matched baseline |
| P1 | Baseline arm: five fresh frozen synthetic cases, no lesson | Raw response bytes, receipts, exact configuration; no gold in prompt |
| P2 | Teach two **disclosed-answer** worked examples in a separate supervised learning context | Guided instruction; not examination |
| P3 | Three guided, corrective examples with explain-back, one competency at a time | Coaching and correction; not independent |
| P4 | Trained arm on the **same five held-out case questions** in separately fresh isolated contexts, with training exposure controlled and recorded; baseline never sees lessons | Matched control-vs-treated assessment; same-question comparison may create contamination if context isn't reset |
| P5 | Examiner independently adjudicates both conditions; Poseidon TRIDENT reviews at milestone | Source/claim, truth/action, false authority, time/effort, semantic contradictions, disagreement and omissions |
| P6 | Human decides to revise, repeat, park or separately approve an adopted instructional configuration | Developmental results never silently become production qualification |

**Important experimental interpretation:** In-context teaching measures **context-conditioned behavior**, not changed neural weights. A versioned summary later inserted into agent instructions measures **instruction/configuration tuning**, not model-parameter fine-tuning. Durable improvement needs a new frozen configuration and later independently held-out re-evaluation.
